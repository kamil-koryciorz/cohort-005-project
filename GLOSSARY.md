# Cadence

An online course platform where Learners buy Courses from Instructors. This glossary firms up the language used on screens, in emails and in code.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys and takes a Course.
_Avoid_: Student, User, Customer

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask to get their money back for a Course purchase, which staff approve or decline by hand.
_Avoid_: Pending Chargeback, Reversal request, Cancellation

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase during which a Learner may submit a Refund Request. It limits submitting, not the staff decision.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Learner may submit a Refund Request for a purchase: inside the Refund Window, under 2 hours watched, under half the lessons completed, not a claimed team seat, and no Refund Request already on that purchase other than a Withdrawn one. It is judged at the moment of submitting.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Refund Amount**:
What the Learner actually paid for the purchase. A Refund always gives back the whole Refund Amount; there are no partial Refunds.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price, partial refund

**Pending**:
A Refund Request that is waiting for a staff decision.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open, Waiting on us

**Approved**:
A Refund Request that staff accepted, so a Refund follows.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request that staff turned down.
_Avoid_: Rejected

**Withdrawn**:
A Refund Request that the Learner called off before staff decided.
_Avoid_: Cancelled

**Refund**:
The money given back to a Learner once their Refund Request is Approved; the Learner no longer has access to the Course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation
